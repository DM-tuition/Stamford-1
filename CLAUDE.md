## 1. SCHOOL CONTEXT

**School:** Altrincham Grammar School for Boys (AGSB)
**Trust:** Hamblin Education Trust
**Type:** UK grammar school, boys only
**Location:** Altrincham, Greater Manchester, UK
**Website:** agsb.co.uk
**School Motto:** *Labor Omnia Vincit* (“Work Conquers All”)
**House System:** Four competing houses — Bradbury, Massey, Stamford, Tatton

### About the AGSB House System

- Reintroduced to AGSB in Summer Term 2019.
- All pupils and staff are assigned to one of the four houses. Siblings are assigned to the same house.
- Each house is managed by a member of staff. Within each year group, elected House Leaders and Deputy Leaders organise events and activities.
- Each house has a Year 13 House Captain and Deputy House Captain who act as student leaders and help coordinate House assemblies.
- Students gain points for their house through activities across the year.
- Purpose: build community across year groups, break down year/form divisions, foster leadership, teamwork, resilience and accountability.

**Stated benefits of the house system (school’s words):**

- Recognise and celebrate success, achievement and excellence
- Increase engagement and inclusion for all pupils — reward endeavour, effort, participation
- Competitive rivalry through house loyalty
- Develop pride, effort, diligence, respect, resilience and responsibility
- Create a sense of belonging
- Build vertical friendship groups, supportive peers and role models
- Provide student leadership opportunities

**House sponsors (each house has a corporate sponsor that funds activities):**

- Stamford: ABC+ Warranty (architectscertificate.co.uk) — local Altrincham structural warranty company
- Tatton: Independents by Sodexo (independentsbysodexo.com) — school catering
- (Bradbury and Massey also sponsored)

House activities & results are shared on the school’s House Twitter page, and latest scores appear on the AGSB home page.

## 3. COLOUR SCHEME & VISUAL IDENTITY

**Primary Colour:** Blue (multiple shades)

- Royal Blue: `#0000CD` / `#1a1aff`
- Steel/Mid Blue: `#4682B4`
- Light Blue: `#ADD8E6`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

**House Badge:** Shield design featuring:

- Horizontal blue and white stripes across the body
- Royal blue chevron/diamond shapes at top and bottom
- White crown symbol at the top centre of the shield
- Banner beneath reading **“STAMFORD”** in capital serif lettering
- Overall aesthetic: bold, heraldic, classic

**No red. No yellow. No green.** Those are rival house colours (Tatton = yellow/black, Massey = green/white, Bradbury = red/white).

**AGSB school brand colours (for reference — the wider school site uses these):**

- School green (dark): `#1a4d2e` / deep forest green — used for headings and accents
- School gold/yellow: `#f5b800` — accent underline colour
- White backgrounds, black body text

**Typography feel:** Strong, clean, confident. Bold headers. Not decorative.

## 4. HOUSE EVENTS & COMPETITIONS

**House Shout:** The flagship house competition. Performing arts / singing event. Stamford’s crown jewel — unbeaten for multiple consecutive years. Mass participation is key to success.
**Sports Day:** Annual athletics competition. Difficult to dominate as it relies heavily on individual talent. Full team sign-up across year groups is the best lever.
**House Assemblies:** Each house leads assemblies for the school. Stamford leaders present to the whole school.
**Student Parliament:** Senior prefect team leads this. Discussed at house leadership meetings.
**Debating / Public Speaking:** A regular house competition. Stamford has historically been strong.
**Drama / Arts events:** Part of the house competition calendar.

## 10. TONE & VOICE GUIDELINES

**For Stamford content (website, social, letters):**

- Confident without being arrogant
- Inclusive — every student in blue has a role
- Legacy-aware — reference the winning history without resting on it
- Direct. No waffle.
- Competitive edge — Stamford wins. That’s just the standard.
- Warm and approachable from leadership — “our door is always open”

**Avoid:**

- Corporate/hollow phrases (“synergy”, “going forward”, “leverage”)
- Overly formal or stiff language
- Anything that sounds like it was written by a committee
