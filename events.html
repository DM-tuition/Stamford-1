House Events

The competitions where points are won and the blue legacy is built. Every one needs you.

The crown jewel

House Shout

Our flagship competition — a mass performing-arts and singing event, and the trophy Stamford has held for five-plus consecutive years.

The secret? Mass participation. The more Stamfordians on stage and in the crowd, the louder we are and the harder we are to beat. If you can sing, act, play, or just bring the energy — we need you.

  • Open to every year group
  • Rehearsals run in the weeks before the event
  • Numbers win it — turn up and bring a friend
“Five years of house shout dominance doesn't come without people stepping up.” — Leo Beesley, 2024/25 Vice Captain

Where Stamford earns its points.

🎤

House Shout

Performing arts & singing. Stamford's crown jewel — unbeaten for years. Mass participation is everything.

🏃

Sports Day

Annual athletics. Hard to dominate as it leans on individual talent — full team sign-up across year groups is our best lever.

🗣️

Debating & Public Speaking

A regular house competition where Stamford has historically been strong. Sharp minds welcome.

🎭

Drama & Arts

Creative house events across the calendar — another stage to score points and show what blue can do.

📢

House Assemblies

Stamford leaders present to the whole school, coordinated by our Captain and Vice Captain.

🏛️

Student Parliament

Led by the senior prefect team and discussed at house leadership meetings — student voice in action.

How it adds up

Points across the whole year.

Students earn points for Stamford through activities right across the year — not just the headline events. Effort and participation count.

Results and activities are shared on the school's House Twitter page, and the latest scores appear on the AGSB home page.
