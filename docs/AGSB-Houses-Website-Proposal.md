# AGSB Houses — Student-Led House Website & App
### A proposal for the school's consideration

**From:** Daniel Stevenson (Stamford House Captain, Year 13) & Ayaan Siddiqui (Vice Captain), on behalf of the Senior Prefect Team
**To:** Mr Birchall (Head of Senior Prefect Team) & the House Managers / SLT
**Date:** 16 October 2026
**Status:** Draft for staff review — *nothing is public, and nothing will go live without your sign-off.*

---

## 1. The idea, in one line
A simple, modern, phone-friendly website that brings **all four houses** — Bradbury, Massey, Stamford and Tatton — into one place: crests, house leaders, the full year's events calendar, and the live points table. It **complements** agsb.co.uk; it does not replace or imitate it.

## 2. Why it's worth doing
- **It drives participation.** The whole point of the house system is mass involvement — and students currently have no single, easy place to see *what's on, when, and how their house is doing*. This fixes that.
- **It's genuinely useful to staff.** The events calendar and live scoreboard are things students ask about constantly; this surfaces them automatically.
- **It showcases student skill** — built by pupils, as a real digital-leadership project.
- **It costs the school £0** — it's a lightweight site on free hosting, with no ongoing bills.
- **Every house is equal.** All four get identical billing, crests and space. This is a school-spirit project, not one house promoting itself.

## 3. What already exists (proof it's real)
We've built a working prototype (currently themed around Stamford as a demo). It already includes:
- An **all-house events calendar** — all ~88 competitions from the official 2025/26 house calendar, searchable and filterable by year group and type.
- A **live points leaderboard** that reads from a **spreadsheet a member of staff controls** (no student can change scores).
- A **countdown to the next house event** and a **"what's on" bar**.
- It **installs like an app** on a phone home screen, and works offline.

*We can show you this privately in ~10 minutes whenever suits.*

## 4. This is clearly UNOFFICIAL — and we want to keep it that way
Every page would be labelled plainly as a **student-led, unofficial project**, not an official AGSB publication. We are asking for the school's **blessing and guidance**, not presenting a finished official product. We will follow whatever branding and safeguarding rules you set.

## 5. Your likely concerns — and our answers
*(We've tried to pre-empt the reasonable worries so this is an easy yes.)*

| Concern | Our answer |
|---|---|
| **Branding / crest use (IP)** | We only use house crests and colours **with your permission**. Everything is marked "unofficial". We will **not** use the official school coat of arms as if it were an official school site, and we'll follow any brand guidance you give. |
| **Safeguarding / pupil photos** | **No pupil photos without the school's consent.** The school supplies and approves any images. No faces go up that you haven't cleared. |
| **Data protection (GDPR)** | **We collect no pupil personal data.** There's no login, no tracking of individuals. The optional "get involved" form simply emails a **staff-monitored inbox**; nothing is stored. Points come from the staff-owned spreadsheet. |
| **User-generated content** | **No public comments, no uploads, no chat.** Nothing a pupil can post publicly. |
| **Accuracy / reputation** | All content comes from **publicly available, official sources** (the school calendar and house pages). Staff sign off before anything is shown, and any error can be corrected in minutes. |
| **Who controls it** | **You do.** The school has full editorial control and can request any change — or take it down entirely — at any time, no questions asked. |
| **What happens when we leave Year 13** | It's deliberately **easy to maintain** (update a spreadsheet — no coding). We'll write a handover guide and pass it to the next Senior Prefect Team, or to the school directly. |
| **Cost / IT burden** | **£0**, and **no load on the school's IT** — it's hosted externally and separate from school systems. |

## 6. How we'd like to proceed (low-risk, staged)
1. **Show you the private preview** (10–15 minutes) — internal link only, nothing public.
2. If you're happy, run a **one-term pilot** behind an internal link, with a **named staff sponsor** overseeing it.
3. Gather feedback, then decide together whether it goes wider.

## 7. What we're asking for
- A short meeting to see the preview.
- Permission to run the pilot, under staff oversight.
- Guidance on branding and any safeguarding requirements you'd want us to meet.

## 8. Our commitment
This is about **house spirit and participation across the whole school** — not personal profile. We'll do it to the standard you expect, hand over control whenever you ask, and stop immediately if you'd prefer we didn't.

> *Thank you for considering it. — Daniel & Ayaan, on behalf of the Senior Prefect Team*
