Student Parliament

Your voice in how AGSB is run. Raise a problem, suggest an improvement, or come to a meeting — every student is welcome.

What it is

How Student Parliament works

Student Parliament is how students help shape the school. It's organised into departments — each one focused on a different part of school life — and led by the senior prefect team alongside staff.

Each department meets regularly to discuss issues students have raised, agree on improvements, and take them to staff. Meetings are open — any student can come along to the department they care about, not just elected members.

This explainer is a first draft — we'll correct the detail once the Parliament info is confirmed.

Two things you can do here

1 · Submit. Send a problem or a suggestion to the right department through the Online Student Parliament form below.

2 · Show up. Check the meeting schedule and come to a session — rooms and dates are listed for every department.

Online Student Parliament

Raise it. Suggest it.

Spotted a problem around school, or got an idea to make AGSB better? Send it straight to the right Parliament department. Real suggestions, from real students, taken to the people who can act on them.

  • Pick the department it relates to — or choose school-wide.
  • Flag it as a problem to fix or an improvement to make.
  • The Parliament team reviews every submission.

Leave an email if you'd like an update — otherwise it goes in anonymously for now.

Submissions go to the Parliament team. Please keep it constructive and respectful.
