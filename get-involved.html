Good to know

Frequently asked

How am I put into a house?
Every pupil and member of staff is assigned to one of the four houses — Bradbury, Massey, Stamford or Tatton. Siblings are always placed in the same house, so families stay together in blue.
How do I earn points for Stamford?
Points come from competing — and from simply taking part — across 70+ events all year. Some events even reward points based on how many Stamfordians turn up, so showing up genuinely matters.
What's the biggest event?
The House Shout — a performing-arts and singing competition where each house performs three times. It's Stamford's crown jewel, and mass participation is the secret to winning it.
How do I become a House Leader?
House Leaders and Deputies are elected within each year group to organise events and rally participation. Express your interest through the form above or speak to the Captains directly.
I'm not especially sporty or musical — is there still a place for me?
Absolutely. The calendar spans debating, chess, quizzes, art, photography, baking, entrepreneurship, eSports and more. Whatever your strength, there's a competition for it.
