FAQ

How am I put into a house?
Everyone is assigned to one of the four houses. Siblings always share a house.
How do I earn points for Stamford?
By competing and by taking part. Some events award points for how many students from each house enter.
What's the biggest event?
The House Shout, a singing and performing-arts competition. Each house performs three times.
How do I become a House Leader?
Leaders and Deputies are elected in each year group. Register interest above or speak to the Captains.
I'm not sporty or musical. Is there anything for me?
Yes: debating, chess, quizzes, art, photography, baking, entrepreneurship, eSports and more. See the events diary.
