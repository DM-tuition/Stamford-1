About the house system

Every pupil and member of staff belongs to one of four houses, and siblings share a house. Houses earn points through competitions across the year, from the House Shout to Sports Day. Each house has a staff House Manager, a Year 13 Captain and Vice Captain, and elected leaders and reps in every year group. The system was reintroduced in 2019.
