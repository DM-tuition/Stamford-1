Welcome to the house

More than a name. A legacy.

Stamford is a tradition woven into the fabric of AGSB. We win the House Shout. We turn up for Sports Day. We debate, we perform — together, across every year group.

  • Every student in blue has a role to play.
  • We reward effort and participation, not just stars.
  • Vertical friendships — Year 7 to Year 13, one team.
  • A winning standard we set ourselves.
