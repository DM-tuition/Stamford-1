How it works

What is the AGSB House System?

Reintroduced in 2019, the house system puts every pupil and member of staff into one of four houses — Bradbury, Massey, Stamford or Tatton. Siblings share a house. Together they compete all year for points, and for the pride of their house.

Each house is run by a staff House Manager. In every year group, elected House Leaders and Deputies organise events, and a Year 13 House Captain and Deputy lead the house and coordinate assemblies. It's designed to build community across year groups and give every student a place to belong — and a chance to lead.

Why it matters

  • Recognises and celebrates success and effort
  • Rewards participation — not just winning
  • Builds pride, resilience and responsibility
  • Creates a sense of belonging across the school
  • Breaks down year and form divisions
  • Gives students real leadership opportunities

Based on the school's own stated aims for the house system.
