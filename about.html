About Stamford

A legacy, a tradition, and a community that spans every year group at AGSB.

Who we are

The dominant house — and the most welcoming.

Stamford is one of four houses at AGSB, reintroduced across the school in 2019. Every pupil and member of staff belongs to a house; siblings share one. We compete all year for points, pride and the trophies.

What sets Stamford apart isn't just the silverware — it's how we win. We capitalise on a diverse range of talents and get everyone involved. Two thirds of the school's major trophy cabinet is blue for a reason.

Our ethos

Every student feels valued. Everyone has a role.

Belonging

A sense of community

The house breaks down year and form divisions, building vertical friendships from Year 7 to 13.

Recognition

Celebrating success

We recognise achievement and excellence — and reward endeavour, effort and participation just as loudly.

Character

Pride & resilience

We develop pride, diligence, respect, resilience and responsibility through fierce, friendly rivalry.

Leadership

Student leaders

Captains, Deputies and elected House Leaders in every year group organise events and lead assemblies.

Participation

Mass turnout

Our edge is numbers. The more Stamfordians who step up, the more we win — Shout, Sports Day and beyond.

Support

Role models

Supportive peers and older students who set the standard and bring the next generation through.

The legacy

Built by people who stepped up.

House Shout dominance doesn't happen by accident. Year after year, Stamfordians have shown up, rehearsed, competed and carried the standard forward.
